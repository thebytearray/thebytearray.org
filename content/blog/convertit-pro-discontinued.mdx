---
title: "Convertit Pro is discontinued"
date: "2026-09-24"
author: Tamim
excerpt: "Pirated copies drove sales to almost zero, so we stopped maintaining Convertit Pro and the free version of Convertit."
---

Convertit Pro is discontinued. Modified and pirated copies started spreading, sales dropped to almost zero, and we can no longer justify the time and cost of keeping the project alive.

We looked at the other option, which was to keep shipping updates to a product that no longer paid for itself. That does not work. Maintenance is not free, and a project with no sales does not earn the hours it takes.

If you made a recent purchase, your refund has already been issued.

The free version of Convertit is stopping too. With no revenue from Convertit Pro, we do not have the resources or the motivation to keep maintaining it.

Thank you to everyone who supported us and the project along the way. We appreciate it. If you still have a question about a purchase, [contact us](/contact/).
