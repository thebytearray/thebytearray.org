OpenLoader is discontinued.

Most people used OpenLoader to sideload apps. That job is now handled better by other mature apps, so we are stepping back rather than ship half-maintained releases.

OpenLoader is no longer available on Google Play or as a download here. Existing installs keep working as they do today.

The source is archived at [github.com/thebytearray/OpenLoader](https://github.com/thebytearray/OpenLoader) (read-only, GPL-3.0). Fork it if you want to carry it forward.

Thanks to everyone who used it.
