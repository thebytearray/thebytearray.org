Hey everyone,

Convertit Pro is back too! ❤️

Last week we [brought Convertit back](/blog/convertit-is-back/) and told you the Pro version would follow in a few weeks. We did not want you to wait that long: Convertit Pro is on Google Play today.

Everything works as before. When we [stopped maintaining the apps](/blog/convertit-pro-discontinued/), it was because pirated copies had eaten the sales that kept the project alive. With the free version back and paying customers returning, both now have a future again. If you ever bought the app, thank you. That is what made this possible.

## What Pro includes

Convertit Pro is the paid version of Convertit. One purchase and every tool unlocks. No subscriptions and no ads. Video compression with your pick of speed, codec, format and resolution. Image conversion from formats like HEIC. Trim, loudness normalization and noise reduction. A ringtone maker, an equalizer, an EXIF cleaner, and media info for any file you throw at it.

All of it runs on your device, the same as the free version. Your files never leave your phone.

## Download

Convertit Pro is on Google Play: [get Convertit Pro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.thebytearray.convertit), or read [more about the app](/apps/convertit-pro/).

The free version of Convertit is still there: [Convertit on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nasahacker.convertit).
