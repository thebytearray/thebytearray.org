Hey everyone,

Convertit Pro is back too! ❤️

Last week we [brought Convertit back](/blog/convertit-is-back/) and said Pro would follow in a few weeks. It is on Google Play now.

Everything works as before. We [stopped maintaining the apps](/blog/convertit-pro-discontinued/) because pirated copies had eaten the sales that kept the project alive. The free version is back, paying customers are returning, and both apps can continue. If you ever bought the app, thank you.

## What Pro includes

Convertit Pro is the paid version of Convertit: one purchase unlocks every tool. No subscriptions, no ads. Video compression with your choice of speed, codec, format and resolution. Image conversion from formats like HEIC. Trim, loudness normalization and noise reduction. A ringtone maker, an equalizer, an EXIF cleaner, and media info for any file you throw at it.

It all runs on your device, like the free version. Your files never leave your phone.

## Download

[Get Convertit Pro on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.thebytearray.convertit), or read [more about the app](/apps/convertit-pro/). The free version is still there: [Convertit on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nasahacker.convertit).
