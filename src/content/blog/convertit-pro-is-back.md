Hi everyone,

Convertit Pro is available on Google Play again as of October 18, 2026. When we [brought Convertit back](/blog/convertit-is-back/) five days earlier, we said Pro would follow within a few weeks.

The app works the same way it did before. We only [stopped maintaining both apps](/blog/convertit-pro-discontinued/) because pirated copies took the sales we depended on. Now that the free version is back and paying customers are returning, we can keep both apps going. If you've bought Convertit Pro at any point, thank you.

## What you get

Convertit Pro is the paid version of Convertit. One purchase unlocks every tool, with no subscription and no ads. You can:

- compress video and choose the speed, codec, format and resolution
- convert images from formats such as HEIC
- trim video, normalize loudness and reduce noise
- make ringtones, use the equalizer and clean EXIF data
- check the details of any media file

Everything runs on your device, just like the free version, so your files are never uploaded.

## Download

You can [get Convertit Pro on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.thebytearray.convertit) or read [more about the app](/apps/convertit-pro/). The free version is still on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nasahacker.convertit) too.
