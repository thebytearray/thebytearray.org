Hey everyone,

Convertit is back! ❤️

A few weeks ago we [announced that Convertit and Convertit Pro were discontinued](/blog/convertit-pro-discontinued/). We're back, self-funded and ready to keep things running. Your messages after our shutdown showed us how much you care about Convertit, and that means a lot to us.

We're done worrying about mods. We're here for the people who love Convertit.

## Convertit Pro

Convertit Pro is still in development and will return in a few weeks.

## Download

Convertit is on Google Play again: [get Convertit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nasahacker.convertit), or read [more about the app](/apps/convertit/).

Thank you for sticking with us!
