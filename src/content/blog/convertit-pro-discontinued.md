> **Update, October 18, 2026:** Convertit Pro is available again. Read [Convertit Pro is back](/blog/convertit-pro-is-back/).
>
> **Update, October 13, 2026:** Convertit is available again, and Convertit Pro is on its way back. Read [Convertit is back](/blog/convertit-is-back/).

Convertit Pro is discontinued. Pirated and modified copies started spreading, sales dropped to almost zero, and we could no longer justify the time and cost of keeping the project alive.

We thought about continuing anyway. Maintenance is not free, and a project with no sales does not earn the hours it takes.

If you bought the app recently, we issued refunds to as many buyers as our balance allowed. If yours has not arrived, [contact us](/contact/) and we will arrange it.

The free version is stopping too. Without revenue from Pro, we do not have the resources or the motivation to maintain it.

Thank you to everyone who supported the project. If you have a question about a purchase, [contact us](/contact/).
