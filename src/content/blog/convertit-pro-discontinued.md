> **Update, October 18, 2026:** Convertit Pro is available again. Read [Convertit Pro is back](/blog/convertit-pro-is-back/).
>
> **Update, October 13, 2026:** Convertit is available again, and Convertit Pro will follow. Read [Convertit is back](/blog/convertit-is-back/).

We've discontinued Convertit Pro as of September 24, 2026.

Pirated and modded copies of the app spread, and sales dropped to almost zero. We can no longer justify the time and money it takes to maintain it. We did consider keeping it going without sales, but the app can't cover its own maintenance costs.

If you bought Convertit Pro recently, we've refunded as many buyers as our balance allowed. If you haven't received your refund, please [contact us](/contact/) and we'll arrange it.

We're also stopping the free version of Convertit, because sales of Convertit Pro paid for its development.

Thank you to everyone who supported the project. If you have a question about a purchase, you can reach us through the [contact page](/contact/).
